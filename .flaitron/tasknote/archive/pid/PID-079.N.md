- **Miss, filed as PID-079.7 [light]🔧 settings-keys-in-core:** the CLI's `Settings.load()` and `ScanEngine.swift:205-210` repeat the same five defaults-key literals. They match today, but a rename would silently break CLI parity. That is the epic's own theme ("Two consumers, one truth").
- **Weighed, accepted (no row):** 079.6's "a Docker-routed group ignores foreign members". `derive(for:)` reports a nil owner when the root routes via `docker stop`. If the stop fails, `KillCore.kill` falls back to POSIX signals, and a foreign member answers `EPERM`. That outcome is `.unkillable` and gets reported truthfully, and Pidlyse never escalates. The cost is at most a badge shown after the click instead of before it, on a rare path. Not worth a child.

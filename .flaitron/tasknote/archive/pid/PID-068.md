**Discovery Notes:**

`logo.png`'s raster is heavily gradient-shaded (~17,000 unique colors, soft bevels/drop-shadow) — confirmed via `magick logo.png -format "%k" info:` and `-unique-colors`. Attempted automated vectorization first (potrace on posterized/color-separated layers) but the color-quantization split the green crosshair and navy circuit dots into muddy antialiased blends rather than clean regions, and would have produced an unmaintainable path-soup SVG. Switched to hand-redrawing: sampled the actual source colors (chip gradient ~#F3F6F8→#AEBBC5, green #4FD873, navy #3E6E96, broom near-white) and reconstructed the same composition (chip w/ pins on all 4 sides, diagonal broom w/ fan head, green ring-crosshair target, circuit traces w/ node dots) as clean SVG shapes + 2 linear gradients. Documented this reasoning in `brand/PROMPTS.md` so a future redesign pass understands why there's no `source:`-plus-converter provenance line.

**Discovery Notes:**

`ScanEngine` owns scan publication and is the appropriate place to retain callbacks until its first published snapshot; `AppDelegate` owns launch and URL-scheme wiring; `PidlyseApp` owns the AppIntent glue. `scan(then:)` deliberately queues a fresh scan, so it is not the narrow cold-start synchronization primitive the task needs. `PID-58.2` established that URL and AppIntent actions share `cleanAllSafe`; this task preserves that shared action and gates only when it may first consume the empty snapshot. `PID-079.3` supplies a deterministic deadline-overridden scan test pattern. No clarification is needed (`--fast`): assume a successful scan publication, even one with zero candidates, is sufficient to permit cleaning; a later clean uses the already-published snapshot without forcing another scan.

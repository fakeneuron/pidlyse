## 🎯 Goal

Lift the scan pipeline, the protected/sweep predicate, and `CleanEvent(item:cwd:)` into `PidlyseCore` so `ScanEngine` and `PidlyseCLI` each make one call, and retire the AGENTS.md "keep the two pipelines in step" convention.

**Implementation Notes:**

- `ScanEngine.swift`: `ScanRequest` (settings captured on main), `ScanSnapshot` (items + four maps), `ScanSource` and `Killer` typealiases. `init(defaults:scanSource:killer:cleanLog:killFeedbackLinger:)` stores each one. - `ScanEngineLifecycleTests.swift` (8 tests): defaults-suite persistence and isolation from `.standard`; the global/scoped ignore split; three in-flight requests collapsing to one follow-up, with completions bound to the scan that started after them; dismissal during flight staying hidden, carried into the next request, and cleared by `rescan()`; kill phases `.killing` → `.gone` / `.survived`, departed rows, linger clear, and the confirmed event landing in the temp log; a newer kill's token surviving the older linger clear, plus the recycled-PID identity check; a respawned verdict taking the replacement out of the sweep; and the `isSweepable` fold (protected / foreign owner / kill in flight) with `sweepableSafeItems` and `cleanAllSafe` killing only the sweepable safe set.

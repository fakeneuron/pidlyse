# PID-069 | respawn-per-identity-watches

[← PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) · 📌 Sidequest (filed 2026-09-24)

## Idea

`RespawnHistory` keeps one watch per signature. Two same-signature rows killed by separate clicks within ~2 s can (a) both read "respawned" off one comeback, because each kill's baseline predates the other's follow-up scan, or (b) miss a respawn when the later kill's watch replaces the earlier one with a later kill-start time. Fix: one watch per killed identity, with each new identity claiming at most one watch; `ScanEngine.kill` then asks per-kill rather than diffing counts. Surfaced as notes in PID-62.5's third external-review pass.

**Implementation Notes:**

`screencapture` CLI requires Screen Recording permission in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording. It's not granted to the terminal running Claude Code. Two capture paths:

**Path A — Quick manual capture (recommended):**
- **Screenshot:** With Pidlyse popover open, press `Cmd+Shift+4`, then press `Space` to enter window-capture mode, click the popover. macOS saves a PNG to Desktop. Move it: `mv ~/Desktop/Pidlyse\ *.png /Users/fakeneuron/Code/pidlyse/docs/screenshots/popover.png`
- **GIF:** Use `Cmd+Shift+5` → "Record Selected Portion", draw around the popover, click Record. Perform the "Clean all" flow, then press the Stop button in the menu bar. macOS saves a `.mov` to Desktop. Convert: `ffmpeg -i ~/Desktop/Screen\ Recording\ *.mov -vf "fps=10,scale=480:-1:flags=lanczos,split[s0][s1];[s0]palettegen[p];[s1][p]paletteuse" /Users/fakeneuron/Code/pidlyse/docs/screenshots/clean-all.gif`

**Path B — Grant terminal screen recording permission, then use `screencapture -i`:**
System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording → + your terminal app → restart terminal.

[← PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) · 🟢 In progress · 🔗 [[PID-62.5]]

## 🎯 Goal

Make popover rows at 320 pt legible: a verbatim single-line PID, a descriptor
that helps tell identical commands apart, and killability badges that don't
read as dead-end buttons.
